# Return & Refund Policy

Last Updated: October 7, 2026

Thank you for using Foxses Studio ("Foxses", "we", "us", or "our").

Foxses provides software, SaaS products, digital services, subscriptions, and business tools. Because our products are primarily digital and subscription-based, refunds and cancellations are handled according to the terms below.

## 1. Digital Services and SaaS Products

Foxses products are delivered digitally. Once access to a paid product or subscription has been provided, the service is generally non-returnable.

Purchasing a subscription gives you access to the selected product or service for the applicable subscription period.

## 2. Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription according to the cancellation options available for the specific Foxses product.

Cancellation will normally prevent future renewals but will not automatically refund payments that have already been processed.

Unless otherwise stated for a particular product or promotional offer, you may continue to use the paid service until the end of your current billing period.

## 3. Refund Eligibility

Refunds are not automatically guaranteed. However, Foxses may consider a refund in reasonable circumstances, including:

* Duplicate or accidental charges
* Unauthorized transactions
* Significant technical issues that prevent access to a paid service and cannot reasonably be resolved
* A billing error caused by Foxses
* Other exceptional circumstances approved by Foxses

Refund requests are reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

## 4. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds may generally not be provided for:

* Change of mind after purchasing a service
* Failure to use the service during the subscription period
* Failure to cancel a subscription before its renewal date
* Services already used during the applicable billing period
* Promotional or discounted purchases where the offer states that they are non-refundable
* Loss caused by incorrect information provided by the customer
* Suspension or termination resulting from a violation of Foxses terms or policies

## 5. Refund Request Process

To request a refund, contact Foxses support and provide:

* Your name
* Account email address
* Product or service name
* Transaction or invoice information
* Reason for the refund request

We may request additional information to verify the transaction and determine eligibility.

## 6. Refund Processing

If a refund is approved, it will generally be issued through the original payment method where possible.

The time required for the refund to appear in your account may depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

Foxses is not responsible for delays caused by banks, card networks, payment processors, or other third-party financial institutions.

## 7. Failed or Duplicate Payments

If you believe you have been charged multiple times for the same transaction or have been incorrectly charged, please contact us as soon as possible.

After verifying the transaction, Foxses may issue an appropriate refund for confirmed duplicate or erroneous charges.

## 8. Service Interruptions

Foxses aims to provide reliable and continuous access to its services. However, temporary interruptions may occur due to maintenance, infrastructure failures, third-party service issues, network problems, security incidents, or circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

A temporary service interruption does not automatically qualify for a refund.

Where a significant and prolonged service interruption is caused by an issue within Foxses's control, we may consider an appropriate service credit or refund at our discretion.

## 9. Changes to Pricing or Services

Foxses may change product pricing, plans, features, or services from time to time.

Changes to future subscription charges will not normally result in a refund for previous billing periods. Where required, customers will receive appropriate notice of material changes.

## 10. Abuse and Fraud

Refund requests associated with fraudulent activity, payment abuse, chargeback abuse, misuse of promotional offers, or violation of Foxses policies may be rejected.

Foxses reserves the right to suspend or terminate accounts involved in fraudulent or abusive activity, subject to applicable law.

## 11. Exceptions

Certain Foxses products or services may have product-specific refund terms, promotional terms, or contractual agreements that differ from this general policy.

Where such terms apply, those terms will take precedence for the relevant product or transaction.

## 12. Contact Us

If you have questions about cancellations, refunds, billing, or payments, please contact Foxses Studio through the contact information provided on our website.
