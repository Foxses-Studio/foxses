# Privacy Policy & Data Security

**Last Updated: October 7, 2026**

Foxses Studio ("Foxses", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide when using our websites, products, applications, and services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices you have regarding your information.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Foxses, we may collect the following types of information:

### Account Information

When you create an account or use our products, we may collect:

* Full name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Company or organization name
* Billing and business information
* Login credentials and authentication information

### Business and Product Data

When you use Foxses products such as our Cloud, Inventory, Invoice, Forms, HR, Template, or other business applications, you may provide information related to your business operations.

This may include customer records, invoices, inventory information, employee information, documents, forms, files, and other data that you choose to store or process through our services.

### Payment Information

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers. Foxses generally does not store complete payment card details on its own servers.

Payment providers may collect and process payment information according to their own privacy policies and security standards.

### Technical Information

We may automatically collect limited technical information, including:

* IP address
* Browser type and version
* Device information
* Operating system
* Usage information
* Log and diagnostic information
* Approximate location derived from technical information

This information helps us maintain, secure, and improve our services.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

* Create and manage your account
* Provide and operate our products and services
* Process transactions and subscriptions
* Provide customer support
* Communicate with you about your account or services
* Send important service and security notifications
* Improve our products and user experience
* Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, and security incidents
* Maintain system reliability and performance
* Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

We do not use your business data for purposes unrelated to providing or improving the services unless permitted by law or otherwise agreed with you.

If you use Foxses to store or process business information, you remain responsible for the data you enter into the service.

Foxses processes this information only as necessary to provide, maintain, secure, and support the relevant service, or as otherwise permitted by applicable law.

You should ensure that you have the necessary rights and permissions to upload or process information through Foxses.

## 4. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.

Depending on the service, security measures may include:

* Encrypted connections using HTTPS/TLS
* Access controls and authentication
* Secure server infrastructure
* Monitoring and logging
* Regular software and infrastructure maintenance
* Restricted access to production systems
* Backup and recovery procedures

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials and should notify us if you believe your account has been compromised.

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain business and transaction records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, meet legal obligations, and maintain security.

When information is no longer required, we may delete or anonymize it in accordance with our operational and legal requirements.

Certain data may remain in backups for a limited period before being permanently removed through our normal backup lifecycle.
