# Foxses Studio

**Foxses Studio** is a software company building cloud-based products for modern businesses.

Our goal is simple: build practical, connected software that helps businesses manage their everyday operations more efficiently.

## Products

Foxses Studio develops and operates multiple products across different areas of business operations.

### Foxses Products

* **Foxses Inventory**
  Inventory and stock management for businesses.

* **Foxses Invoice**
  Invoicing and billing tools for managing business transactions.

* **Foxses HR**
  Human resource and employee management platform.

* **Foxses Templates**
  Ready-to-use business and productivity templates.

### Studio Products

* **Cloud Forms**
  Cloud-based forms for collecting, managing, and organizing business information.

* **Support System**
  Customer support and helpdesk software for managing customer requests and support operations.

## Our Vision

We want to build a connected ecosystem of business software where companies can use different tools from one company while keeping their workflows simple and organized.

Instead of building one product for one problem, Foxses Studio is gradually building a broader software ecosystem covering areas such as:

* Business Operations
* Finance & Invoicing
* Inventory Management
* Human Resources
* Data Collection
* Customer Support
* Productivity

## Our Approach

We focus on building software that is:

* **Simple** — Easy to understand and use.
* **Practical** — Built around real business needs.
* **Connected** — Designed to work as part of a larger ecosystem.
* **Scalable** — Suitable for growing businesses.
* **Cloud-based** — Accessible from anywhere.
